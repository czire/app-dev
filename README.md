# 🎭 My Favorite Series: Money Heist (La Casa de Papel)

## 📖 About the Series
**Money Heist** (*La Casa de Papel*) is a Spanish heist crime drama series following a mysterious man known as "The Professor" who recruits a group of eight unique specialists to carry out an ambitious, multi-day heist on the Royal Mint of Spain and later the Bank of Spain.

---

## ❤️ Why I Like It
* **Brilliant Heist Strategies:** Masterfully executed plans with contingencies for every possible outcome.
* **Intense Suspense & Pacing:** High stakes and non-stop tension keep you on the edge of your seat.
* **Complex Character Dynamics:** Deep emotional connections, flaws, and motivations within the crew.
* **Iconic Themes & Soundtrack:** Unforgettable motifs like the song *"Bella Ciao"* and the iconic red jumpsuits and Salvador Dalí masks.

---

## 👥 My Favorite Characters

| Character | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **The Professor** (Sergio Marquina) | The mastermind behind the heists; brilliant, detail-oriented, and strategic. |
| **Tokyo** (Silene Oliveira) | The fiery, unpredictable narrator who acts as a loyal and fearless fighter. |
| **Berlin** (Andrés de Fonollosa) | The charming, sophisticated, yet ruthless second-in-command. |
| **Nairobi** (Ágata Jiménez) | The passionate, highly skilled quality control expert with a big heart. |
