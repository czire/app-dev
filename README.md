# My Favorite Series: Money Heist (La Casa de Papel)

## About the Series
Money Heist (La Casa de Papel) is a Spanish show about a man called "The Professor" who plans huge heists on the Royal Mint of Spain and the Bank of Spain with a team of criminals named after cities.

## Why I Like It
- Very smart plans and clever plot twists.
- Fast-paced and keeps you guessing what happens next.
- Great characters with interesting backstories.
- Iconic masks, outfits, and music (like "Bella Ciao").

## My Favorite Characters

| Character | Description |
| --- | --- |
| The Professor | The mastermind behind the whole plan. Very smart and prepared. |
| Tokyo | The narrator of the show who is brave and unpredictable. |
| Berlin | The second-in-command who is calm, charismatic, and unpredictable. |
| Nairobi | Super passionate, strong-willed, and cares a lot about the team. |

## My Favorite Quote
> "In this world, everything is governed by balance. There’s what you stand to lose and what you stand to gain."
> — The Professor

## Rating
9.5/10 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

## Conclusion
Money Heist is one of my favorite shows because of its exciting storyline, great suspense, and memorable characters.
